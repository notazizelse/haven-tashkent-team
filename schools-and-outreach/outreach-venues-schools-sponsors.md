## RULES BEFORE YOU SEND ANYTHING

1. **Phone or Telegram first, email second.** Email is the document they forward to their boss, not the thing that gets you the meeting.
2. **Subject lines lead with their benefit, not your need.** "100 teenagers in your building" beats "Request for venue."
3. **Under 200 words.** Anything longer doesn't get read.
4. **One ask per message.** Never two.
5. **Always end with a question they can answer in one word or one line.** "Can I call Thursday?" beats "Let me know your thoughts."
6. **Never mass-send.** Change at least the name and one specific detail every time. *(HQ's guide: "Ten customized emails is a million times better than a thousand mass emails.")*
7. **Reply within 24 hours**, always. *(HQ's guide.)*
8. **Attach the one-page PDF info sheet** to every formal email.
9. **Follow-up cadence:** Day 0 → Day 3 (phone) → Day 7 (final) → stop. Three touches maximum.

# PART F — CLASS TALK SCRIPTS

Once a school says yes, this is what you deliver. Use the 25-minute version when the teacher gives you a full lesson, the 10-minute version otherwise. Take a laptop with a game already loaded.

## THE 25-MINUTE CLASS TALK — full script

> **[0–2 min]**
> *"Salom. Men [ISM], [YOSH] yoshdaman, [MAKTAB]da o'qiyman. Men o'qituvchi emasman — sizlar bilan tengdoshman."*
>
> **[2–5 min — play a game on the projector. Don't talk over it.]**
> *"Mana bu o'yin. O'ynab ko'ringlar."* Let one student play for 60 seconds.
>
> **[5–8 min]**
> *"Bu o'yinni bir kishi ikki soatda yasagan. Hech qanday oliy ma'lumotsiz. Siz ham qila olasiz."*
>
> **[8–14 min]**
> *"Haven — 14–15-noyabr kunlari bo'ladigan ikki kunlik tadbir. Dunyo bo'ylab 200 ta shaharda bir vaqtda. 13 dan 18 yoshgacha. Butunlay bepul. Ovqat ham bizdan. Ikki kun ichida o'z o'yiningizni yasaysiz, u internetda bo'ladi, havolasini hammaga yubora olasiz. Va sertifikat olasiz."*
>
> **[14–18 min — answer the three fears BEFORE they're asked]**
> *"Uch savolni oldindan javob beray:*
> *Birinchi — «men dasturlashni bilmayman». Zo'r. Ko'pchilik bilmaydi. Birinchi soatda hammamiz birga o'rganamiz.*
> *Ikkinchi — «men hech kimni tanimayman». Do'stlaringiz bilan keling — bitta jamoada bo'lasizlar, kafolat beramiz.*
> *Uchinchi — «bu men uchunmi?». Agar o'yin o'ynasangiz, chizsangiz, musiqa yozsangiz yoki shunchaki qiziqsangiz — ha."*
>
> **[18–22 min — QR on screen]**
> *"Hozir ro'yxatdan o'tinglar. Bir daqiqa vaqt oladi. Keyin emas — hozir."* **Stand and wait while they scan. Do not move on.**
>
> **[22–25 min]**
> *"Kim shu sinfdan Haven ambassadori bo'lishni xohlaydi? Sizga plakat, havola va sertifikat beramiz."* Take their Telegram handle on the spot.
