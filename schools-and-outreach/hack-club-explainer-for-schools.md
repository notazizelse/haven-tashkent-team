# 🇬🇧 ENGLISH

## WHAT IS HACK CLUB?

**Hack Club is a registered non-profit organisation in the United States that helps teenagers learn to build things with technology.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | 2014, by Zach Latta and Jonathan Leung |
| **Legal status** | Registered 501(c)(3) non-profit (The Hack Foundation), Tax ID 81-2908499, Burlington, Vermont, USA |
| **Scale** | Over **100,000 teenagers** have taken part · **1,500+ school clubs** worldwide |
| **Cost** | Free for every teenager. Permanently. Hack Club has never charged a participant. |
| **Transparency** | All organisational finances are published publicly |
| **Recognition** | Founders named to *Forbes* 30 Under 30 (2016). Profiled by *The Wall Street Journal* and the *Today Show*. Among its donors is Elon Musk, who gave $1 million in 2021. |

Hack Club describes itself as *"the world's largest non-profit movement of teenagers making cool projects."* It provides curriculum, training and funding to student-run clubs and events in schools across the world.

---

## WHAT IS HAVEN?

**Haven is a game-development event for 13–18 year olds, taking place in approximately 200 cities worldwide on the same weekend: 14–15 November 2026.**

**Tashkent is one of those cities — for the first time.**

Over two days, participants build their own video game from nothing and publish it online. No programming experience is required; the first session on Saturday morning teaches everyone from zero.

Hack Club has run this format before at scale:
- **Daydream** — 100 student-led game jams in 100 cities
- **Scrapyard** — events in more than 70 cities
- **Campfire** — a single-weekend global game jam with around 10,000 participants

---

## WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Saturday morning** | A guided workshop. Every participant builds the same small game together, step by step. |
| **Saturday, before lunch** | **Every participant publishes a working game online.** Even those who had never written code that morning. |
| **Saturday afternoon – Sunday** | Participants improve their game and add their own ideas. Optional workshops in pixel art, sound design and game design run alongside. |
| **Sunday afternoon** | A showcase where participants play each other's games, followed by certificates for everyone who completed a project. |

Participants leave with a game that has a public web link they can share with family, and an international certificate.

## SAFETY AND SUPERVISION

Hack Club has a formal written safeguarding policy that all organisers must follow, published at **hackclub.com/safeguarding-policy**. For Haven Tashkent this means:

- ✅ **Adult supervision at all times** throughout the event
- ✅ A **designated safeguarding officer** on site, clearly identifiable, with published contact details
- ✅ A **first-aid trained person** present whenever participants are
- ✅ **Closed venue** — only registered participants may enter
- ✅ **Check-in and check-out logs** for every participant
- ✅ **Written parental consent** required before any student may attend
- ✅ **Zero tolerance** for alcohol, tobacco or any prohibited substances
- ✅ **No overnight stay.** Participants go home each evening and return the next morning.
- ✅ A **formal incident reporting procedure** to Hack Club headquarters
- ✅ Organiser phone numbers available to parents before, during and after the event

A separate guide for parents is provided to every registered family.

## ЧТО ТАКОЕ HAVEN?

**Haven — мероприятие по созданию видеоигр для подростков 13–18 лет, которое проходит примерно в 200 городах мира в одни и те же выходные: 14–15 ноября 2026 года.**

За два дня участники создают собственную видеоигру с нуля и публикуют её в интернете. Опыт программирования не требуется — первое занятие в субботу утром обучает всех с нулевого уровня.

Hack Club уже проводил подобные мероприятия в большом масштабе:
